Reference evidence & modelling assumptions +

The supplied plan guides the 8.20 × 5.05 m baseline. Column diameter of 0.20 m is an estimate from its symbols. Its source and the DAE model’s physical scale remain unverified.

Hanan & Wonorahardjo, Figure 7 (p. 16), provides separate section guidance: foot 1.56 m, body 1.67 m, marked roof height 2.75 m. Its 3.86 × 5.60 m dimension chains refer to lower support/grid lines; the section preset provisionally maps these to a floor rectangle. The roof section station is unspecified.

Roof equations, wall angles, beam sections, support arrangement, stone dimensions, and slider ranges are modelling assumptions. The DAE was inspected as a visual reference; this prototype generates new geometry and does not import its mesh. Presets are studies, not measured reconstructions. The rule order is computational, not a documented construction sequence. No structural safety is implied.
